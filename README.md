# 🐎 Horse Race Betting Simulation (C++ Console App)

A console-based horse racing and betting simulation written in C++. The player deposits money, chooses a horse to bet on based on odds, watches a race simulation, and wins or loses money based on the outcome.

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

1. The player enters a starting deposit.
2. A list of horses with different odds is displayed (lower odds = higher chance of winning).
3. The player selects a horse and places a bet.
4. The race begins – horses (represented by numbers) move forward randomly, with higher chances for horses with lower odds.
5. If the chosen horse wins, the player earns money based on the odds.
6. After each race, the player chooses whether to continue betting or exit the game.

## ⚙️ Technologies

- C++
- Console-based interface
